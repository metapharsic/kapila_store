Recent Recipes Shelf

Your last 5 used dishes appear as one-click chips above the search box, so you never retype the same recipe twice.

How to use

1
First time — use search. Type the dish name in the search box, find it in the dropdown, and click "+ Add". The dish is now remembered.
2
Next visit — one click. The shelf appears automatically above the search box. Click any chip to add that dish instantly.
3
Already added? The chip turns green with a checkmark. Clicking it again does nothing — it's already in today's plan.
The last 5 dishes you used are saved in your browser and persist across sessions. Monday's regular dishes are always one click away.

How to read it

1
Coverage badge — "7/10 in stock" means 7 of 10 ingredients are fully covered at the current plate count. Green = full, amber = partial, red = none.
2
Green row — the Store has enough of this ingredient. No action needed.
3
Amber row — stock exists but not enough for the current plate count. Consider reducing plates or checking with the Store.
4
Red row — zero stock. The Store cannot issue this item. Arrange a transfer or substitute before submitting.
Colours recalculate live as you move the plate slider or use the steppers — drag left to see more rows turn green.

 All four controls — steppers, slider, input box, and presets — stay in sync. Change any one and the rest update immediately.

How to use

1
−25 / −10 — click to reduce plates by 25 or 10. Ingredients rescale immediately.
2
+10 / +25 — click to increase. Each click is additive — click +10 three times to add 30 plates.
3
Type directly — click the centre number box and type any value from 1 to 500 for exact control.
4
Preset buttons — 50, 100, 150, 200 snap to a round number in one click. Highlighted in amber when active.
5
Drag slider — smooth continuous control. All other controls follow.
The minimum is 1 plate. The system will never allow 0 or negative — the stepper stops at 1.

How to use

1
Confidence (default sort) — HIGH → MED → LOW. Puts the most reliable items at the top so you review the best predictions first.
2
Qty sort — biggest volume items first. Useful for checking bulk ingredients like oil, flour, and dal.
3
A–Z sort — alphabetical order. Fastest when you know the item name and want to find it immediately.
4
Filter box — type any part of an item name to narrow the list. The counter updates to show how many are visible vs selected.
5
Confidence badges — HIGH = item ordered in 75%+ of this weekday's history. MED = 50–75%. LOW = below 50%.
Sort and filter only affect what you see. All checked items compile regardless of display order — unchecking removes an item from the indent.

Reading the badges

R
Recipe only — this ingredient comes exclusively from the dishes you added in the Recipe Planner panel. Quantity is scaled by your plate count.
T
Trend only — this item was pulled from your department's historical indent data. It is not part of any recipe you added today.
R+T
Both sources — the ingredient appears in a recipe AND in the trend history. The system adds both quantities together into one line item.
Items marked R+T deserve a second look — the combined quantity can be higher than expected. Review the total before compiling.

Stock Warnings in Compile Bar

What each warning means

Red — "N out of stock" — N items have zero remaining stock. The Store physically cannot issue them. Contact the Store Manager or substitute the item before submitting.
Amber — "N low stock" — N items have some stock but less than your requested quantity. A partial issue may happen.
3
No tags = clean — all items in your indent are fully covered by current stock. You can proceed with confidence.
4
Warnings don't block you. You can still click Preview & Compile. The Store Manager will see the shortfalls in their approval screen.
The draft timestamp ("draft 2m ago") next to the count confirms your last auto-save time. Your work is safe even if the browser closes.

How to use the modal

1
Source column (Src) — R / T / R+T shows the origin of each line. Helps the Store Manager trace items.
2
Uncheck to exclude — untick any item to remove it from submission. It stays visible but greyed out.
3
Edit quantities — click any Qty box to type a new value. Stock status updates immediately.
4
Review in Manual Tab — loads all checked items into the manual entry form for further editing before submitting.
5
Compile & Submit Directly — sends the indent straight to the Store Manager's approval queue.
6
Item count footer — shows how many items are checked vs total. Excludes unchecked items from the submission.
Items you exclude here are not submitted. The Store Manager only sees the checked items. Use exclusions for items you will source through a different channel.

Draft Auto-Save

Every change you make is saved automatically to your browser. If you close the tab or navigate away, your entire plan is restored when you return.

How draft saving works

1
Always on. Every time you add a dish, move the slider, check/uncheck a trend item, or edit a quantity — the draft saves instantly.
2
Persists across sessions. Close the browser, restart your computer — your draft is still there when you reopen the Indent tab for that department.
3
Per-department isolation. The TIFFINS draft and the STAFF draft are stored separately. Switching departments never overwrites your work.
4
Timestamp in compile bar. "draft 2m ago" = last saved 2 minutes ago. "draft just now" = most recent change is captured.
5
Reset clears the draft. Clicking ↺ Reset wipes the draft for that department. You'll start with a blank plan next time.
Drafts are saved in your browser's localStorage. Clearing browser data or using a different device will not restore the draft — complete and submit your indent on the same device.

Kapila IMS: Store Manager Workflow

A guided tour of the operational features and screens available to the Store Manager on a daily basis.

1

The Store Manager Dashboard

The Store Manager's home screen is designed for rapid action. It highlights exactly what needs attention right now, stripping away high-level executive analytics in favor of ground operations.

  • Offline Sync Status: Shows if any operations were performed offline and are waiting to sync with the database.
  • Top KPIs: Instantly shows how many indents are pending, how many items are low on stock, and what is expiring in the next 3 days.
  • Shift Handoff Notes: A persistent sticky note where the outgoing manager can leave instructions for the incoming shift manager.
  • Activity Feed: A real-time timeline of what just happened (e.g., "North Indian requested 5kg Rice").
  • Quick Modules: Massive buttons to quickly jump into core modules: Available Stock, Purchase Order, Store Issuance, and Indent Request.
2

Managing Indent Requests

Every morning and evening, departments submit "Indents" (material requests). The Store Manager reviews and approves these requests here.

  • Auto-Indent Recommendations: The system flags if a department is requesting unusually high quantities compared to historical averages.
  • Recipe Planner Integration: The manager can see exactly why a department needs 10kg of Rice (e.g., "They are producing 200 plates of Biryani").
  • One-Click Approval: Reject, modify, or approve requests with a single click. Approved indents are immediately sent to the Issuance queue.
3

Store Issuance (Fulfilling Requests)

Once an indent is approved, the physical goods must be handed over to the department staff. This screen handles the actual inventory deduction.

  • Pre-filled Quantities: The issuance form is automatically filled out based on the approved indent. The manager just verifies the physical handover.
  • Smart Unit Conversion: The system handles math automatically. If the chef requested "5 Liters" of oil, but the master stock measures in "Milliliters", the system accurately calculates the financial cost.
  • Cost Tracking: Every issuance automatically calculates the monetary value of the goods leaving the store, tying inventory costs directly to the department's daily P&L.
4

Receiving Purchases (GRN)

When the supplier truck arrives, the Store Manager must ingest the new inventory and log the invoices.

  • AI Invoice Scanning: The manager can simply upload a photo of the physical invoice/receipt. The built-in AI will read the receipt, extract the items, quantities, and prices, and automatically populate the Purchase Order form.
  • Local Fallback: If the hotel loses internet, the system falls back to an offline local scanner to ensure work never stops.
  • Price Benchmarking: When entering a new price, the system immediately flags if the supplier is charging more than the historical average for that item.
